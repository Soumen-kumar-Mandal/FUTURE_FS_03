Our story

Started at a stove that never really turned off.

Adda Bari began the way most good food does — in a home kitchen, feeding whoever showed up. Our grandmother cooked for the neighbourhood before she ever cooked for money. When we opened these doors, we kept her recipes untouched: same mustard paste ground by hand, same slow-cooked mangsho, same patience.

No fusion, no shortcuts. Just the food a Bengali household makes when it wants you to stay a little longer.
